The Architecture of My Career

From core computer science to spatial reality.

With a BSc in Computer Science and a foundation in interior design, I operate at the intersection of raw code and spatial perception. Currently pursuing my MSc in Building Information Modelling (BIM) at UWE Bristol, I specialize in computational BIM, automated data interoperability, and advanced 3D architectural modeling. Highly capable of bridging the gap between traditional construction management and digital asset automation, I have a proven track record of architecting BIM-to-Web pipelines, enforcing strict ISO 19650 data governance, and maintaining high-quality digital records. I am also capable of bridging the gap between heavy 3D authoring tools (Revit, AutoCAD) and web-based data structures (IFC, APIs, JSON) to ensure robust, single-source-of-truth information management for large-scale estates.

I am currently doing a researcg that accesses BIM-Web based visualisation systems to improve design communication and stakeholder decision making. My research focuses on the integration of BIM data with web-based platforms to enhance collaboration, visualization, and real-time decision-making in architectural and construction projects. By leveraging advanced technologies such as Three.js, WebVR, and cloud-based data management systems, I aim to create immersive and interactive experiences that facilitate better understanding and engagement among project stakeholders.
